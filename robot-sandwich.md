
# Robot makes a *jelly* sandwich (true story)
- There is a fully charged robot
- The robot is facing a counter that has the following items
    - a jar of jelly
    - a clean butter knife
    - a loaf of wrapped bread

# Steps
1. Unwrap the plastic wrapper from the bread
2. Take the first two slices of bread and place them side by side
3. Unscrew the jar of jelly on the counter
4. Pick up the knife in one hand
5. Use the knife to scoop some jelly from the jar
6. spread the jelly on the knife onto one slice of bread until it is covered in jelly
   <br> 6.5 if there's not enough jelly on the knife to fully cover the top facing slice of bread, then repeat steps 5 and 6 until it is fully covered <br>
    6.5.1 if there's excess jelly on the knife, hand the knife to me so I can eat it, and return to the counter to continue sandwich-making
7. IF the knife is still in hand after fully covering the bread, set the knife down on the counter .
8. Pick up the unjellied bread 
9. Set the unjellied bread on top of the jellied bread
10. Screw the lid of the jelly jar back onto the uncovered jelly jar .
11. Pick up the jelly sandwich and hand it to me .
